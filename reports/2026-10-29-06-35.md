2. **Use Clear Visual Demonstration Over Static Stills:** Show products solving a specific annoyance within the first 3 seconds of video (e.g., tangled wires snapping into order, a dark room lighting up). Practical demonstrations retain viewers longer than slideshow images.
3. **Respond to Every Comment Within the First Hour:** Promptly answer questions regarding delivery times, color variants, or quality directly in the comments, and guide interested users to the link in your bio or caption. Fast creator replies signal active community management to the platform.
