## 4. Three Practical Ways to Improve Engagement

1. **Use Relatable Local Scenarios in Video Hooks:**  
   Instead of generic greetings, start videos with relatable Pakistani context (e.g., *"When you have only one working socket in the room during load shedding..."* or *"Walking through Sunday Bazaar with your phone in your pocket..."*). Relatable hooks stop the scroll faster.

2. **Incorporate Interactive Story Stickers (Polls & Quizzes):**  
   Use Instagram Story stickers like "Which color would you pick?" or "Do you still use Micro-USB? (Yes / No)". User interactions tell platform algorithms to show your content to more followers organically.

3. **Respond to Every Comment Within the First Hour:**  
   Prompt responses to comments (e.g., answering "Price please?" with *"Sent you the product link in DM!"*) not only provide good service, but double the total comment count, signaling strong activity to the platform's distribution algorithm.
